Delete your account — Sayad app

You can delete your Sayad account and all its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap your photo on the home screen to open your profile.
  3. Tap "Delete my account" at the bottom, type the confirmation word and confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Email support.sayad@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete Sayad account". We process the request within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The account and profile information.
  • The fishing diary and its photos.
  • Posts, photos, videos, comments and likes.
  • Reports you sent and your block list.
  • The consent log and usage data linked to your account in our database.

What is kept

  • Spots and shops you suggested that were approved: they stay in the app without any link to your name.
  • Backups: erased automatically within 30 days.
  • Data Google holds directly (payments, analytics, ads), under its own policies.

Important: your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
