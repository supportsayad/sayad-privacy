Sayad Community Rules

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The Sayad community is a place for anglers to share their catches and experience respectfully. These rules are part of the terms of use.

Welcome

  • Photos of your catches, tips, questions and real experiences.
  • Respect, even when you disagree.
  • Respect for the sea: responsible fishing and releasing small fish.

Not allowed

  • Abuse: insults, harassment, threats or bullying.
  • Hate: demeaning anyone for their origin, religion, sex, disability or anything else.
  • Sexual, violent or shocking content.
  • Spam and advertising: links, phone numbers, repeated ads or selling without permission.
  • Illegal fishing: posting or promoting fishing with explosives, poison or banned nets, fishing in protected areas or closed seasons, or selling protected species.
  • Private information: posting someone's phone number, address or photo without their consent.
  • Impersonation or misleading information about how dangerous or safe a place is.
  • Off-limits places: suggesting spots on private property, military zones or closed ports.
  • Other people's content: photos or videos you do not own the rights to.

How we enforce the rules

  • Automatic text checks before posting.
  • A "Report" button on every post and comment, and a "Block" button to hide a user from you.
  • Content is hidden automatically after several reports from different people, pending review.
  • For violations: removal of the content, then a posting ban, then account deletion for serious or repeated cases.

Urgent issues or appeals: support.sayad@gmail.com
